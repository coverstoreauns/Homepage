Why "universal fit" keeps failing Australian cars

A loose, one-size dash mat or seat cover is cut to an average shape that no actual vehicle has. It bunches around the gearstick, gaps at the dash edges, and slides under braking — because it was never built for the car it ended up in.

Coverstore.au works the other way: every pattern starts from the vehicle's own make, model and year, so vents, sensors, stitching lines and airbag panels line up the way the factory intended, and the material itself is chosen for Australian heat and UV rather than a milder overseas climate.

  • Airbag-aware seat and dash patterns, not sewn over safety seams
  • UV-stable, Australian-made material tested for local heat
  • 500+ vehicle patterns, updated as new models arrive
